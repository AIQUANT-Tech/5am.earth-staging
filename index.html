5am.earth

AboutFoundation, Charter 2025

5am.earth is an agricultural trust platform, conceived as a continuous loop of verified insights that connects smallholder farmers to the financial and market systems that have historically been closed to them. It is operated as a neutral foundation — not a data broker, not a platform — and is sponsored by a coalition of agri-entrepreneur networks, satellite intelligence providers, blockchain identity infrastructure and field implementation partners across South & Southeast Asia, sub-Saharan Africa, and Latin America.

The foundation extracts the insights farmers need — about their land, their practices, their market — and verifies them once so they can be trusted everywhere. Farmers own the result. They decide what is shared, with whom, and when. Insights are not a feature of the product; insights are the path to farmer prosperity, and the product is the infrastructure that makes them shareable, trustable, and finally, useful.
