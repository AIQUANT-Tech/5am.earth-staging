06 · Finance use case

Seedstars × 5am.earth — a complete farmer financial lifecycle on Cardano.

Seedstars serves as finance intelligence and underwriting partner — ingesting 5am.earth verified data to generate AI-driven credit scoring, structure loan offers, and feed repayment data back into the trust layer. Starting with a 1,000–5,000 farmer pilot cohort, with input loans and working capital of $100–$500 initially, scaling to $1,000.

The infrastructure is designed to be a public good — no single commercial partner can own or control it. This is what makes it trustworthy to every party simultaneously — commercial, institutional, and sovereign. Each party can trust it precisely because none of them controls it.

08 · The investment

$2.5M (10,000,000 ADA) from the Cardano Treasury over 18 months.

The Cardano Treasury funds the open infrastructure and 5am.earth's role in connecting the AE network to Cardano — the trust layer, satellite oracle, DID/SSI identity stack, dApp integrations, AE onboarding support, and Foundation governance. The commercial activity this infrastructure serves is independently funded and already operating at scale. 5am.earth is the open layer through which that activity generates Cardano protocol revenue for the first time. Structured across three milestone-gated payments (5M ADA on approval, 2M at M1, 3M at M2), with Cambodia and Kenya confirmed as funded-period expansion countries; the Foundation reaches self-sustainability by M3 (December 2027) and does not return to the Treasury for operating costs thereafter.
